🛡️ Safety Protocols

Flood Safety Guidelines

⛰️

Move to Higher Ground

At the first sign of rising water, evacuate to higher elevations immediately. Don't wait for official orders if danger is visible.

🚫

Avoid Flood Water

Never walk or drive through floodwater. Just 15 cm of moving water can knock you down; 30 cm can sweep a vehicle away.

🎒

Emergency Kit Ready

Keep a kit with 3 days of water, food, torch, first-aid, medications, documents, and a battery-powered radio.

⚡

Turn Off Electricity

Switch off all electrical appliances and the main power supply before floodwater enters your home to prevent electrocution.

📞

Stay in Contact

Inform family of your location. Keep your phone charged. Monitor official broadcasts and emergency alerts at all times.

🏠

Protect Your Home

Move valuables and furniture to upper floors. Use sandbags at entry points. Seal gaps around doors and windows.

💊

Prevent Disease

After floods, boil drinking water, avoid contact with contaminated water, and watch for symptoms of waterborne illness.

🗺️

Know Your Route

Plan evacuation routes in advance. Know the location of shelters. Practice the plan with all family members.

ℹ️ About FloodWatch

Built for Communities at Risk

FloodWatch is a public-awareness platform designed to help individuals, families, and local authorities quickly access flood risk information. Our database covers flood-prone regions, historical event data, and direct emergency contacts.

Data is compiled from government meteorological departments, disaster management authorities, and historical flood records.
